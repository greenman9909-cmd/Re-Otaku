Website privacy

Updated 27 November 2026. This page describes the RE:OTAKU download website. App behaviour and optional connected services are described separately in the project documentation.

Download counts

Clicking a download records the selected platform and a random browser identifier with our Supabase service. The identifier is stored in your browser’s local storage as reotaku_download_visitor so the service can limit repeated counts from the same browser and platform in one day. The public site shows aggregate totals; download requests are not verified installs.

Public reviews

If you submit a review, your chosen name, rating, review text and submission date are sent to Supabase and displayed publicly. Do not include private information in a review. To request removal, contact the maintainer through the GitHub project and identify the review without sharing other personal information.

Local preferences

Your language selection is saved locally in this browser as reotaku_site_language. You can remove these preferences and the random download identifier by clearing this site’s browser data.

Third-party services

Vercel hosts this website, Supabase provides download statistics and reviews, and GitHub hosts the APK downloads and project documentation. Requests to these providers include standard connection information such as your IP address and user agent. Each provider handles that information under its own policies.

What loads on this page

The site serves its font and screenshots locally. It does not add advertising scripts or a third-party analytics SDK. Opening GitHub links takes you to GitHub. The app may use additional services when you choose to connect an account or play a title.
